Start here: choose how your QR works

PixelPing brings QR content, design, printing, and hosted Smart Cards into one workflow. This manual follows the controls in the app. For a specific problem, visit the Help Center.

Static or Dynamic?

Mode reference
ModeWhat a scan opensWhat can change later
StaticThe content encoded directly in the QR, such as a website address, phone number, or text.The encoded content cannot change after export. To change it, generate and distribute a new QR.
Dynamic — EvolutionA hosted PixelPing route that redirects to a website or displays a Smart Card.Edit the existing route in the Command Center to keep its URL and printed QR. Hosted routes require internet access and active Evolution access.

Static QR images do not have a PixelPing subscription expiration. A website they point to can still move or go offline. Static generation can work offline after the app has loaded and cached successfully; sign-in, purchases, cloud sync, and Dynamic routing need a connection.

Make your first QR

  1. Open the generator and select a content tab, such as Website, Phone, Email, SMS, or Coupon.
  2. Choose Static or Dynamic where supported, then enter your actual content. Placeholder examples are not saved values. Some content types are Static only.
  3. Give the QR a useful name. Adjust the colors, corner style, and center icon or custom QR logo. Keep the code readable and its clear margin intact.
  4. For Dynamic, select Save & Activate and wait for Route active. Correct any highlighted field before continuing.
  5. Save a reusable design with Save to My Codes, then download or preview your print. Scan the exported file and a physical sample before distributing it.

Save, activate, and start a new QR

Control reference
ControlWhat it does
Save & ActivatePublishes the current Dynamic route. It does not download a file or save a design to My Codes.
Save to My CodesStores a named snapshot of the QR content, design, and print settings in your account library. It does not activate a route.
Update Saved CodeUpdates the saved design you opened. It does not change its existing live destination.

Forgot an icon or changed the print message?

You can keep editing without deleting everything. Visual changes such as a QR center icon, colors, or Smart Print label appear in the next export. Update the saved design, or use Save as New for a separate version. Download or print again to obtain the new appearance; an already printed image cannot change.

Changing the actual Phone, SMS, Email, or Coupon content in the generator can create a new Dynamic draft. To update a Smart Card that people already scan, use Edit Page in the Command Center instead. Check the route URL before exporting.

Recent and My Codes

Recent holds your last five saved or exported designs. My Codes holds up to 50 deliberately saved designs across devices when signed in and synced. Search by name or destination. Open restores a design to the canvas; More actions provides Rename, Duplicate, and Delete Saved Design.

A duplicated Dynamic design needs its own activation and new exported QR. The original route stays separate. If you see “sync pending,” keep this browser’s data and reconnect before assuming the design is available on another device.

When changing an opened design, a Save Changes / Discard Changes / Cancel prompt protects unsaved work. Deleting a saved design removes its snapshot; it does not delete its live route.

Smart Cards: Phone, SMS, Email, and Coupon

A Dynamic Phone, SMS, Email, or text Coupon creates an automatic Smart Card: a mobile page with your business name, description, action details, and the appropriate button. A Coupon containing a website URL can follow the website-routing workflow instead. Static actions use their encoded content directly.

Card reference
CardVisitor action
PhoneCall now opens the device dialer. No call is placed automatically.
SMSSend a message opens the messaging app with the recipient and any prepared message. The visitor chooses whether to send.
EmailSend an email opens a mail app or an app chooser with the recipient and any subject and body. Nothing is sent automatically.
CouponCopy Coupon Code copies the displayed code. An optional Redeem Offer button opens the supplied HTTPS link.

The Smart Card is generated for you; it is not a separate page builder. The business monogram and action artwork are part of the layout. A center logo on the QR itself is separate from the Smart Card. The Powered by PixelPing footer remains on hosted Smart Cards.

Three different kinds of message

  • Business description: the introduction displayed on the Smart Card.
  • Prepared SMS or email message: text placed into the visitor’s messaging or mail app.
  • Smart Print label: headline and message displayed beneath the QR on an export or print. It does not set the prepared email or SMS body.

For field sizes, see character limits. Use a complete international phone number with + and the country code when needed. Supported +1 numbers display with spacing and punctuation while the action still uses the normalized number.

Manage live routes in the Command Center

  1. Open the Command Center while signed into the account that owns the route. Select Sync to refresh access and route information.
  2. Find your route using search and the page controls. Use Copy to obtain its URL; a friendly Rename helps you identify it.
  3. For a website route, choose Reroute Link. For a Smart Card, choose Edit Page, update its fields, and select Save Page Changes. These edit the existing route without replacing its QR.
  4. Wait for confirmation, then rescan the original QR. If the save result is uncertain, Sync and reopen the editor before retrying.
  5. Use Save to My Codes or Open in Canvas when you need a reusable design. A live route record and a saved design are separate items.

Deleting a route is permanent

Delete Route stops downloaded and printed copies from working and cannot be undone. A saved design can remain in My Codes, but it cannot bring the deleted route back. Duplicate its design, activate a new route, and replace the old QR wherever it was distributed.

Scans, activity, and notifications

The Command Center displays Dynamic scan totals, account activity, and notice delivery status. Static scans do not pass through PixelPing’s hosted routing service. Opening a Dynamic route while testing can contribute to its activity. Marking notices read does not erase the activity history.

Email notices may arrive during testing when delivery is enabled. There is currently no user-facing Pause Email control. This manual does not describe planned controls as available features.

For branded routes, follow Custom Domain Setup. For billing or paused routes, see Account & Recovery.

Design, download, and print

Select a print layout and physical size that match your intended use. Smart Print labels use a bold, centered headline with a regular, centered message below it. Paragraph breaks are preserved. The combined headline and message limit is 1,024 characters, including the line break between fields.

Format or control reference
Format or controlHow to use it
PNGA raster image for digital use or printing at adequate resolution. Avoid enlarging a small image until its squares blur.
SVGA vector export that scales cleanly. Useful when a printer or designer needs to resize the artwork.
PDFUses the print preview workflow. Select Print / Save as PDF, then your browser’s Save as PDF destination.
Preview & PrintOpens a preview first. Review the layout before opening the browser print dialog.

SVG, PDF, and premium design tools require the corresponding paid access. If a Dynamic route is not active or has unsaved route changes, the export workflow can ask you to activate or save before continuing.

  1. Keep a clear margin around the code and strong contrast between its data and background. Large logos, pale colors, glare, and a curved surface can make scanning harder.
  2. Check the actual downloaded file, not just the on-screen canvas. Confirm that its recipient, website, or card is correct.
  3. Print a sample at the final physical size and test it on the intended material with more than one phone when possible.
  4. For PDF, review paper size, margins, scaling, and page count in the browser print dialog. Adjust settings if the browser changes the layout.

No color combination or export format guarantees a scan in every environment. Keep the successful sample as your reference.

The Arsenal: put a QR to work

Open the Arsenal in the generator to choose a play and load its setup instructions. Complete the actual destination and any required fields, review the access needed, and activate a Dynamic route before exporting it.

  • Table or counter: link to your menu, booking page, or review destination. Keep the action label clear.
  • Contact card: offer a phone, text, email, or vCard action and test the receiving phone’s handoff.
  • Printed campaign: use a Dynamic route when you need to change a website destination later. Manage it in the Command Center.
  • Offer: use a Coupon Smart Card with a clear code, a short note, and an optional redemption link.

The Arsenal supplies starting points, not completed business content. Test your own links and permissions before sharing. Return to the generator.

Before you share

  • Confirm that a Dynamic route shows Route active and opens from the exported QR.
  • Test the action button, including the prepared SMS or email text, without sending anything you do not intend to send.
  • Keep the reusable design in My Codes and confirm sync if you need it on another device.
  • Test the final printed size and material. Keep the original QR when testing an in-place route update.
  • For Route unavailable, missing access, downloads, or DNS issues, use the Help Center.
