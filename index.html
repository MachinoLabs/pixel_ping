Why I built PixelPing: Rebellion.

Not long ago, I created a "free" QR code on one of the most popular generator websites. A week later, the code mysteriously stopped working. I went back to the site and realized they had secretly made it a Dynamic QR code, meaning there are servers, redirects and other fallible processes involved. My link was now a hostage until I paid a surprise monthly subscription (I did not). I wanted to know if it was just me that got fooled, so I searched for QR generator reviews on sites like Reddit, Quora, and Facebook and found that this bologna is happening to a lot of marketers and small business owners. That yanked my chain. So I said F-it and built my own.

PixelPing is different. Your codes are generated 100% locally with no redirects... ever. I intentionally made them static, which means you build it once, and it works forever. No hidden subscriptions, no hostage situations, no BS. I built the tool I wished existed, and now I'm sharing it with you to offer an alternative.

About PixelPing

PixelPing creates fast, offline-first QR codes tailored for field operations.

  • • Runs instantly without downloads
  • • 100% offline-ready and ad-free
  • • High Error Correction ensures codes survive dirt and damage
  • • Free tier includes standard codes; Master Key unlocks Pro payloads and styling

How to Use

  • Step 1: Select what you want your code to do (Open a URL, share Wi-Fi, etc.) and enter the details.
  • Step 2: Use the Smart Print Label to name your code so people know what to scan.
  • Step 3: Customize the aesthetics and structure to match your brand.
  • Step 4: Save the code digitally, or Print it directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this app collect data?
No. PixelPing is completely client-side. Your payloads and settings stay strictly on your local device.

Can I use this without Wi-Fi?
Yes! Once you load the app for the first time, it is fully functional offline.

What do I get with the Pro version?
Pro unlocks advanced payloads (like Wi-Fi, vCards, and Crypto), custom styling, the Smart Print Label feature, and much more.
