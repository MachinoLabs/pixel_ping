Why I built PixelPing: Rebellion.

Not long ago, I created a "free" QR code on one of the most popular generator websites. A week later, the code mysteriously stopped working. I went back to the site and realized they had secretly made it a Dynamic QR code, meaning there are servers, redirects and other fallible processes involved. My link was now a hostage until I paid a surprise monthly subscription (I did not). I wanted to know if it was just me that got fooled, so I searched for QR generator reviews on sites like Reddit, Quora, and Facebook and found that this bologna is happening to a lot of marketers and small business owners. That yanked my chain. So I said F-it and built my own.

PixelPing is different. Your codes are generated 100% locally with no redirects... ever. I intentionally made them static, which means you build it once, and it works forever. No hidden subscriptions, no hostage situations, no BS. I built the tool I wished existed, and now I'm sharing it with you to offer an alternative.
