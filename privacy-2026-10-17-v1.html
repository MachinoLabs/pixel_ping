Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Version 2026-10-17-v1

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelPing and its sole-proprietor operator handle information when you use our QR generator, account features, Dynamic routes, Smart Action Pages, and payments. It also explains what happens when someone scans a PixelPing Dynamic QR. Contact support@senditin.app with privacy questions or requests.

1. Information you provide

  • Account information: account identifiers, email address, and profile or sign-in information provided through Clerk and any sign-in provider you choose.
  • QR content and designs: destinations, messages, business or contact details, offers, logos, design settings, saved names, Recent Codes, My Codes, and brand settings you choose to enter or save. Some QR payloads can contain personal information.
  • Purchase and access records: product and subscription information, payment status, transaction references, customer identifiers, entitlements, license information, and relevant billing events.
  • Communications and agreement records: support messages, abuse reports, account activity notices, and policy agreement information such as account ID, version, document fingerprints, and acceptance time.

2. Payments and sign-in

Stripe collects and processes payment information through its checkout interface. PixelPing’s own checkout code does not receive or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes. We receive the transaction, customer, subscription, and payment-status information needed to provide purchases and handle billing. Stripe may collect additional technical and fraud-prevention information under its own policy.

Clerk processes authentication, session, and account data, including sign-in email and profile information. If you choose a third-party sign-in provider, information is also handled by that provider. Authentication cookies and related security processing are part of signing in.

3. Scans, visits, and technical data

When a browser or scanner requests a Dynamic route or Smart Action Page, the hosting infrastructure receives technical request information such as IP address, request URL, browser or user-agent information, and time. Network information may reveal an approximate region; PixelPing does not request a scanner’s device GPS location for its scan counter. Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers may process technical information for delivery, security, diagnostics, and abuse prevention.

PixelPing maintains a total scan count for each Dynamic route for account reporting. The current application scan counter does not create a visitor-by-visitor profile, store raw scanner IP addresses with the count, or report individual scanner identity to the QR owner. IP addresses and signed-in account IDs are also used for rate limiting protected icon requests. A scan count reflects route requests and may include repeat, automated, or preview requests; it is not a verified count of unique people.

Generating a Static QR takes place in the browser. PixelPing does not receive a scan count when someone scans a Static QR that goes directly to another site. If you save its design to your account, the saved payload is processed as account content.

4. How we use information

We use information to provide authentication, create and deliver QR features, maintain routes, synchronize account work, fulfill purchases, manage access, report scan totals, provide support and service notices, prevent abuse, secure the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We use only the information reasonably needed for these purposes.

Where data-protection law requires a lawful basis, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, comply with legal obligations, or pursue legitimate interests in operating and securing it, subject to applicable rights. Where consent is legally required for an optional activity, it must be obtained separately; accepting the Terms or acknowledging this Policy is not a blanket consent.

5. Browser storage and external resources

PixelPing uses local storage, session storage, and a service-worker cache for preferences, recent work, local design copies, pending synchronization, license-related state, returning between pages, and offline generator resources. Some local copies remain until you clear browser storage or remove the item. Clearing storage can remove unsynchronized work and offline access; shared-device users should sign out and consider clearing stored work.

The app loads necessary scripts or styles from providers such as Clerk, Stripe, Tailwind’s CDN, and jsDelivr. These providers receive the technical information needed to deliver their resources. The current app does not include Google Analytics or advertising-tracking scripts. That does not mean the service is anonymous: authentication, payments, infrastructure security, and Dynamic scan totals still involve processing described here. Optional analytics or marketing technologies would require an updated disclosure and any legally required choices before use.

6. Who receives information

  • Clerk: authentication and account management. Clerk privacy information.
  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions, and related fraud prevention. Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: website delivery, Workers, account and route storage, private asset storage, and security. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Other necessary providers: resource delivery and support communications, as needed to operate the service.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to respond to valid legal process, to protect safety and rights, to investigate abuse, or as part of a lawful business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Public QR content and destinations

QR images can be read by anyone who obtains them. Smart Action Pages and active routes can be accessed by people with their addresses; an unlisted URL is not a confidentiality control. Phone numbers, email addresses, business details, coupon information, and logos you place on public pages are visible to their visitors. Avoid putting passwords, payment-card details, or other confidential information in a QR or public page.

After a scan redirects or a visitor opens an external destination, that destination’s operator handles the visit under its own policies. A QR creator may use independent tracking on the destination site; PixelPing does not control that processing. QR creators are responsible for appropriate notices and permissions for their own content and data use.

8. Storage, retention, and deletion

Account and route information is stored using the service providers described above; local work may also be stored in your browser. Providers may process information in the United States or other countries in which they operate. Applicable data-transfer safeguards and legal requirements depend on the provider and processing involved.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the account and routes, provide requested services, maintain security and acceptance records, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. We do not promise indefinite storage or guaranteed restoration. Recent Codes retains a rolling list; My Codes follows the displayed account limit. Browser data follows your device and browser storage settings.

You can remove saved designs or routes using the available controls. Deleting a design does not delete its active route. Route deletion disables the route, and a minimal reservation or deletion record may remain to prevent reuse of a previously printed address. Billing, security, agreement records, backups, and provider records may be retained where necessary or legally required; deleting an item does not guarantee immediate removal of every technical copy.

For an account-wide deletion request, contact support@senditin.app. We may verify your identity and explain any information that must be retained. Removing a sign-in account alone should not be treated as confirmation that a subscription was canceled or every route and billing record was erased; request confirmation from support.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on it; or appeal a decision. Contact support to make a request. We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law and may ask for identity verification. You may also complain to the appropriate data-protection authority. Not every right applies to every record or jurisdiction.

You can choose whether to create an account, use optional paid features, upload a logo, publish contact information, or follow an external link. Browser controls can restrict cookies and storage, but doing so may prevent sign-in, checkout, synchronization, or offline features from working.

10. Security

PixelPing uses account authentication, access checks, and provider security features to help protect information. No internet service, device, or storage system is completely secure. Protect your credentials and license keys, avoid sensitive data in public QR content, and contact us promptly about a suspected security issue.

11. Children

PixelPing accounts and purchases are intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact support so we can investigate and take appropriate action. QR creators must comply with applicable children’s privacy requirements for their own content and destinations.

12. Policy updates and contact

We may update this Policy when practices or legal requirements change. The effective date and version identify the current notice. We will provide appropriate notice of material changes and obtain any consent required for new processing. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@senditin.app.
