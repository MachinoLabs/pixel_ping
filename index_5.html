About this app

PixelPing creates simple, fast, offline-first codes.

  • Runs instantly without downloads
  • No ads, no data tracking
  • Fully offline after first load

How to Use / Instructions

  • Step 1: Select your payload type (URL, Wi-Fi, Phone, etc.) from the dropdown.
  • Step 2: Fill in the generated form. The app automatically translates it into machine code.
  • Step 3: Customize your colors, shapes, and center icons. The matrix uses High Error Correction to survive masking.
  • Step 4: Click "Save Code" to download a crisp, scalable image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this app collect data?
No. Everything stays completely on your local device.

Can I use this without Wi-Fi?
Yes, once the app loads the first time, it works completely offline.
