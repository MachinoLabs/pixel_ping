Save, activate, or duplicate

Why does a saved QR say Route unavailable?

Save to My Codes saves a design; Save & Activate publishes its Dynamic route. In the generator, complete all required fields, select Save & Activate, and wait for Route active. If a field is highlighted, correct it and try again. A blank Business Name is not the placeholder text shown in the box.

For an existing route, refresh and select Sync in the Command Center. Confirm you are signed into its owning account and that Evolution access is verified. Use Check Again if offered. An unavailable route can reflect an activation, connection, account, or deleted-route issue; repeatedly clicking Save is not a substitute for reading its feedback.

Can I add an icon after activation?

Yes. Adjust the QR artwork or Smart Print label, then update the saved design or choose Save as New. Download again to receive the changed artwork. An existing printed QR keeps its appearance. To change a live Smart Card’s content at the same URL, use Command Center → Edit Page → Save Page Changes.

What happens when I delete or duplicate?

Delete Saved Design removes the snapshot only. Delete Route permanently stops the live QR, including printed copies. A deleted route cannot be restored by saving its design again. Duplicate creates a separate design; a Dynamic copy needs a new active route and new QR copies.

My saved design is missing on another device

Check the account on both devices, reconnect, and Sync or Refresh the library. My Codes holds up to 50 saved designs; Recent contains only the last five saved or exported designs. “Sync pending” means cloud sync has not finished. Keep the originating browser’s data until it completes.

Character limits and required fields

These limits apply to hosted Smart Cards and their fields. The prepared email/SMS message is separate from the printed label. The generator’s validation and counters guide entry; whitespace and line breaks can count toward the limit.

If the app reports an invalid email address, enter an actual recipient; the example shown in an empty field is only a placeholder. If a business name is required, fill the Business Details field even when the print headline already contains a name.

Phone, message, email, and coupon actions

  • Phone: opens the dialer; the visitor confirms the call.
  • SMS: opens the messaging app with the number and prepared text. It does not send automatically.
  • Email: may show an app chooser before a compose window. Select a configured mail app and check the recipient, subject, and body. A missing mail account must be configured on that device.
  • Coupon: Copy Coupon Code copies the exact displayed code. Paste into a note to verify it. Redeem Offer opens the optional link; PixelPing does not automatically apply a discount on the destination website.

A Smart Card loading correctly and an action opening the intended app are separate checks. Test both. The device and selected app control the final compose or dial screen.

Downloads, print preview, and scanning

Where is my PDF?

PDF export opens the print preview workflow. Choose Print / Save as PDF, then select Save as PDF in the browser’s print dialog and choose a location. Preview & Print does not print immediately. Review paper size, margins, scaling, and page count.

The download is missing or will not open

Check the browser’s downloads list and download permissions. PNG and SVG download as files; PDF uses the print dialog. Open the actual file before sharing it. If a private browsing session handles the download unexpectedly, try a normal browser window and compare the result. Do not assume an unfamiliar temporary filename proves the QR data is broken.

The camera cannot read my QR

Check contrast, the clear margin around the code, logo size, and the exported resolution. PNG can print well at adequate resolution; SVG scales without raster blur. Try a larger physical code, a flat surface, and less glare. Test the final printed sample with another phone.

The camera reads it, but the destination fails

The image is being decoded. Check its website address or Dynamic route instead. A Static website QR can point to a page that has moved. For Dynamic, check activation, owning account, Evolution status, and any custom-domain verification. See activation help.

Plans, billing, and Account & Recovery

Nomad is $0 for standard Static tools. Rebellion (Master Key) is $99 one-time for premium design and Static tools. Evolution is $39/month and includes Rebellion features while subscribed plus hosted Dynamic services. Check the current pricing and checkout before buying.

I paid, but my access has not updated

Sign into the purchasing account, refresh, and use Sync. Open Account & Recovery in the Command Center and select Refresh status. If payment or subscription history is unverified, contact support@senditin.app before buying again. Include the purchase email and receipt reference, never payment-card details or passwords.

Can I pause email notifications during testing?

There is currently no user-facing pause or off button for email notices. Account activity shows notices and their delivery status when enabled. Mark all read changes their read state; it does not stop future emails.

Custom Domain Setup

Use an unused subdomain you control, such as qr.example.com, for branded Evolution routes. Root/apex domains require separate support. Do not replace the DNS records that serve your main website or email.

  1. In the Command Center, select Add Custom Domain. Enter the hostname and select Generate DNS Records.
  2. At your DNS provider, add the exact records returned by PixelPing. Match each Type, Name / Host, and Value / Target. Copy record value helps avoid typos; your DNS provider may expect only the subdomain portion of the name.
  3. For Cloudflare-managed customer DNS, use a DNS-only (grey cloud) CNAME during this initial integration. Use the values in your own setup screen rather than a guessed target.
  4. Return to PixelPing and select Check Verification Status. Review hostname validation, TLS certificate, and whether the CNAME points to PixelPing.
  5. Wait until Custom Domain Active. DNS propagation and certificate checks can take time; there is no fixed activation guarantee. Then test a route using the branded hostname.

If verification stays pending, compare every record with the setup screen and check for conflicting records at that exact subdomain. If the certificate is still pending, allow time and check again. Send support the hostname and displayed statuses if it remains unresolved. A registered hostname alone is not an active domain.

Frequently asked questions

Does Static mean offline scanning?

A Static QR encodes its own data. Text can be read without visiting PixelPing, but a website still needs a connection and its own hosting. The app must first load and cache successfully for supported offline generation. Dynamic routes, account checks, and sync need internet access.

Can I change a printed code?

Static encoded content and printed artwork cannot change. An existing Dynamic route’s destination or Smart Card can be updated through the Command Center while keeping the same route URL and QR. Editing a saved design alone does not update that live route.

Will a QR logo change my Smart Card branding?

The QR center logo is part of the image you export. Smart Cards use their automatic business-name, monogram, and action layout, including the Powered by PixelPing footer. These are separate surfaces.

Where can I read the terms and privacy information?

Read Terms and Privacy for the current policies. Avoid placing secrets in QR content: anyone who receives a QR may be able to read its payload or open its destination.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@senditin.app with your browser/device, content type, Static or Dynamic mode, steps taken, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot of any feedback. Include the route URL only if you are comfortable sharing its content. Never send passwords, authentication codes, API keys, or full payment details.
