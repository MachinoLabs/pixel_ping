⚡ What is PixelPing?

PixelPing is a rebellion against the subscription-hostage model of the modern internet. Most QR code generators let you create a "free" code, secretly make it dynamic, and then break your code a week later unless you pay a surprise $20/month subscription.

We do things differently. PixelPing creates fast, offline-first codes tailored for field operations. By default, our codes are generated 100% locally on your device. They don't use tracking servers, they don't redirect, and they will work forever. Build it once, own it forever.

🌐 Connect Your Custom Domain

To make your QR codes and shortlinks look professional (e.g., links.yourwebsite.com/promo), you need to connect your custom domain to PixelPing. This tells the internet that you authorize us to route your traffic securely.

Step 1: Initialize Your Domain

  1. Log into your PixelPing Command Center.
  2. Click the 🌐 Add Custom Domain button at the top of the dashboard.
  3. Enter the domain you want to use.
    Pro Tip: We highly recommend using a subdomain like links.yourwebsite.com or qr.yourwebsite.com. Most registrars do not allow you to route a root domain (like yourwebsite.com) to a third-party app.
  4. Click Generate DNS Records. PixelPing will securely talk to our servers and generate the exact records you need. Leave this window open!

Step 2: Log Into Your Registrar

  1. Open a new browser tab and log into the company where you bought your domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Wix, Squarespace, etc.).
  2. Find your domain and look for a button that says DNS Settings, Manage DNS, or Advanced DNS.
  3. Look for the button to Add New Record.

Step 3: Add the Routing Record (CNAME)

This record tells the internet to send people who scan your code directly to PixelPing's servers.

  1. Click Add New Record in your registrar.
  2. Under Type, select CNAME.
  3. Go back to your PixelPing tab and click Copy next to the Name / Host in Step 1. Paste it into your registrar.
  4. Click Copy next to the Value / Target in Step 1. Paste it into your registrar.
  5. Hit Save.

Step 4: Add the Verification Record (TXT)

This record acts as a secret password to prove to our security systems that you actually own this domain.

  1. Click Add New Record in your registrar again.
  2. Under Type, select TXT.
  3. Go back to your PixelPing tab and click Copy next to the Name / Host in Step 2. Paste it into your registrar.
  4. Click Copy next to the Value / Target in Step 2. Paste it into your registrar.
  5. Hit Save.

🎉 You're Done!

Go back to PixelPing and click "I've Added These Records."

Note: It usually takes about 5 to 15 minutes for your registrar to update the global internet directories. Once those records sync, your custom domain will be live, secure, and ready to scan!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Static and Dynamic?

Static (Local) codes are built entirely on your device and are never sent to a server. Because they don't redirect, they can never be tracked or broken. Dynamic codes are saved to our cloud engine, allowing you to update where the code points even after you've printed it. Dynamic codes track scans but require an internet connection.

Does this app collect my data?

No. If you generate a Static code, your payloads and settings stay strictly on your local device. We only store data when you explicitly choose to create a Dynamic link so we know where to route the traffic.

Can I use this without Wi-Fi?

Yes! Once you load the main generator page for the first time, the core tool caches in your browser. You can generate local static codes (like Plain Text or WiFi) entirely offline.

What do I get with the Pro version?

The free tier handles basic URLs and phone numbers perfectly. Upgrading to Pro unlocks the Command Center dashboard, dynamic cloud routing, custom domain mapping, advanced payloads (vCards, Crypto, Calendar), and the ability to customize your code's shape, colors, and logos.

Are there hidden subscription fees?

Never. We hate subscriptions. The Master Key is a one-time purchase for lifetime access to the Pro tier.
